# NIST AI RMF: the php2shell risks it governs

For AI used to find flaws and AI used to report on them: who governs it, what it can reach, and how to switch it off.

**Full name** NIST AI Risk Management Framework 1.0 (AI 100-1) · **Publisher** US National Institute of Standards and Technology · **Version** 1.0, January 2023 · [the official text](https://doi.org/10.6028/NIST.AI.100-1)

## Clauses and controls

|---|---|---|---|
| **GOVERN-1.1** | Legal and regulatory requirements involving AI are understood, managed and documented | *no risk mapped* |  |
