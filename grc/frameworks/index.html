Frameworks: the law, the standards and the controls each risk maps to

20 frameworks, 120 clauses and controls, 95 of them mapped to at least one risk. A mapping says which control a holder of the risk will be asked about, by an auditor, a regulator or a board; it does not say the control is in place. Control titles are this desk's short paraphrase; each framework links to its official text.

Law and regulation

Binding on the organisations in scope: a gap here is a legal exposure, not a choice.

FrameworkVersionControls usedRisksWhy it is here
GDPR
General Data Protection Regulation, Regulation (EU) 2016/679
2016, in force 25 May 20186 of 63A PHP application that executes an attacker's code exposes the personal data it holds; whoever controls that data answers for its security and for notifying a breach.
NIS2
Directive (EU) 2022/2555 on a high common level of cybersecurity across the Union
2022, transposition deadline 17 October 20246 of 88Essential and important entities (energy, health, digital infrastructure, public administration and more) must manage vulnerabilities, handle incidents and report significant ones; management bodies answer for it personally.
Cyber Resilience Act
Regulation (EU) 2024/2847 on horizontal cybersecurity requirements for products with digital elements
2024; reporting obligations apply from 11 September 20264 of 46php2shell is among the first critical flaws in a ubiquitous open-source component since the Act's reporting duty began: a manufacturer whose product ships PHP must handle the flaw and report an actively exploited one; the PHP project is the kind of open-source steward the Act treats lightly.
DORA
Digital Operational Resilience Act, Regulation (EU) 2022/2554
2022, applies from 17 January 20255 of 65Banks, insurers and other financial entities running PHP, or relying on an ICT provider that does, must identify, protect, detect, respond and report under one regime, and manage the provider as a third-party risk.
EU AI Act
Artificial Intelligence Act, Regulation (EU) 2024/1689
2024; general-purpose model obligations apply from 2 August 20253 of 44Frontier models found these flaws, and the same capability in other hands finds the next ones: the Act asks providers of general-purpose models with systemic risk to assess and mitigate exactly that. PHP October 2026 (php.oct.2026.newsroom.sgit.ai) tracks PHP news for one month. This month that is one story, the two critical PHP security flaws, followed as evidence: every claim with a status and its sources, every source captured, dated and hashed into a private vault. Built and run by one editor and a team of software agents; the roles and the method are public, and the site is rebuilt from the vault, never written by hand.
