# CISA KEV and BOD 22-01: the php2shell risks it governs

The public test of 'exploited': a CVE enters the catalogue on evidence of exploitation in the wild, and US federal agencies must then remediate by a due date. php2shell is not in it as of the vault's last capture.

**Full name** CISA Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog and Binding Operational Directive 22-01 · **Publisher** US CISA · **Version** BOD 22-01, November 2021 · [the official text](https://www.cisa.gov/news-events/directives/bod-22-01-reducing-significant-risk-known-exploited-vulnerabilities)
