Power users: start here

This site is being built in public, every day, by Dinis Cruz and a team of AI agents. We are looking for people who will use it as it grows: read what is new when we ask, as the kind of reader you are, and tell us what works and what does not. A few minutes an ask, sometimes several asks a day while the story is moving; answer the ones you have time for. If you have not seen agentic workflows doing real work, this is a close look at one.

Start here See the latest ask Send feedback Join

Start here: your first fifteen minutes

  1. Read the front page: Two minutes: what happened, in plain words, and the four boxes at the top (fix, severity, attackers, sourced).
  2. Pick the reader closest to you: A reader for each job, from a curious reader to a CISO, a DPO or a developer. Your reader gives you a short briefing and a reading path: follow it for five minutes.
  3. Try the site on your phone: Now, the phone app: what you need to know for one, five, ten or thirty minutes.
  4. See how it is made: Two people and a team of AI agents, each with a page, their tasks and what they shipped; what they ran and when is on the runs page.
  5. Do the latest ask: Below on this page: what is new, what to do, one or two questions. A few minutes.
  6. Tell us what you think: In the WhatsApp group, by replying to the email, or with the form below. Short is fine; a screenshot is welcome; anything that contradicts something else on the site is gold.

That is all there is to joining in: after this, each ask tells you what to look at.
