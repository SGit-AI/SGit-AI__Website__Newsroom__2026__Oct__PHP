# Power users: start here

This site is being built in public, every day, by Dinis Cruz and a team of AI agents. We are looking for people who will use it as it grows: read what is new when we ask, as the kind of reader you are, and tell us what works and what does not. A few minutes an ask, sometimes several asks a day while the story is moving; answer the ones you have time for. If you have not seen agentic workflows doing real work, this is a close look at one.

[Start here](#start) [See the latest ask](#ask-001) [Send feedback](#feedback) [Join](#join)

## Start here: your first fifteen minutes
- [Read the front page](../index.html): Two minutes: what happened, in plain words, and the four boxes at the top (fix, severity, attackers, sourced).
- [Pick the reader closest to you](../start/index.html): A reader for each job, from a curious reader to a CISO, a DPO or a developer. Your reader gives you a short briefing and a reading path: follow it for five minutes.
- [Try the site on your phone](../now/index.html): Now, the phone app: what you need to know for one, five, ten or thirty minutes.
- [See how it is made](../team/index.html): Two people and a team of AI agents, each with a page, their tasks and what they shipped; what they ran and when is on the runs page.
- [Do the latest ask](../collaborators/index.html#ask): Below on this page: what is new, what to do, one or two questions. A few minutes.
- [Tell us what you think](../collaborators/index.html#feedback): In the WhatsApp group, by replying to the email, or with the form below. Short is fine; a screenshot is welcome; anything that contradicts something else on the site is gold.

That is all there is to joining in: after this, each ask tells you what to look at.

## What happens to what you send
- It is encrypted in your browser to the collaborators vault's key (`sha256:e3ff50abd96816ef`) and posted to a write-only lane on that private vault (`58526ytp`). The lane answers the same whatever arrives, so this page can say it was sent, never that it arrived.
- It stays in that vault: not on this site, not in its repository, not in its telemetry. The editor and the conductor read it. Feedback becomes work on [the board](../admin/board/index.html) in the team's words, never with your name unless you agree.
- No phone number is collected here. WhatsApp is the editor's own group; he asks for your number himself.
- Anyone can type anything into a form, so a request is read by a person before anyone is added or written to.
