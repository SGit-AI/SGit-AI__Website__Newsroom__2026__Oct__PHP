## Verification: what runs and what does not

- **Runs on every build:** every fact cites only sources that exist in the vault's manifest; every link resolves; every section's text is hashed and versioned; the site is byte-identical when rebuilt.

- **Runs when an agent writes a fact:** the status word is chosen from the sources it cites, and a fact never gets a better word than its sources give it.

- **Not yet run:** an independent verifier that re-reads every fact against the saved text of its sources, and a second model reading the vault. Both are on the list; neither has happened, and this line will change when they do.
