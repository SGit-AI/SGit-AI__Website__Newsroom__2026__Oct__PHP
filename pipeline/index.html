Verification: what runs and what does not

  • Runs on every build: every fact cites only sources that exist in the vault's manifest; every link resolves; every section's text is hashed and versioned; the site is byte-identical when rebuilt.
  • Runs when an agent writes a fact: the status word is chosen from the sources it cites, and a fact never gets a better word than its sources give it.
  • Not yet run: an independent verifier that re-reads every fact against the saved text of its sources, and a second model reading the vault. Both are on the list; neither has happened, and this line will change when they do.

Every fact has its own timeline

Each fact has a page of its own: when each of its sources was published, when it was saved, when it entered this site and every change of wording or status since, the sources it rests on as a graph, and the briefings it appears in. Start from every claim and follow the timeline link on any row.

PHP October 2026 (php.oct.2026.newsroom.sgit.ai) tracks PHP news for one month. This month that is one story, the two critical PHP security flaws, followed as evidence: every claim with a status and its sources, every source captured, dated and hashed into a private vault. Built and run by one editor and a team of software agents; the roles and the method are public, and the site is rebuilt from the vault, never written by hand. One of the SGit Newsroom's story sites: up from here are October 2026, the year and the newsroom, whose own section on the story is here. Part of the sgit.ai network.
