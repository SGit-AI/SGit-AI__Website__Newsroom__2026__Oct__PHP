#### What the page says

_Curator's reading of the capture._

The Register: Amazon Threat Intelligence detected an attempt to exploit PHP2Shell on 17 October, four days before patches, through MadPot, its honeypot network, from an anonymised IP address; Amazon says it is unclear how the actor got exploit information under embargo. Hadrian reported on 21 October that PHP2Shell was being "exploited in the wild at scale". The article reports attempts; it names no organisation as compromised.
