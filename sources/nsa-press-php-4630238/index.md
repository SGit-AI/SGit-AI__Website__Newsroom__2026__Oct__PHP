# NSA Advisory: Critical Patches Recommended for PHP Vulnerabilities

**US National Security Agency (NSA)** · [https://www.nsa.gov/Press-Room/Press-Releases-Statements/Press-Release-View/Article/4630238/nsa-advisory-critical-patches-recommended-for-php-vulnerabilities/](https://www.nsa.gov/Press-Room/Press-Releases-Statements/Press-Release-View/Article/4630238/nsa-advisory-critical-patches-recommended-for-php-vulnerabilities/) · published 2026-10-21 · added by `collector-gov` · slug `nsa-press-php-4630238`

Press release dated Oct. 21, 2026 (no time given). Nicknames the bugs 'PHP2Shell'. Recommends patching, inventory, enhanced logging, replacing EOL PHP; 'If an immediate update is not feasible, it is recommended that organizations take the system offline until it can be fully mitigated.' Says the risks 'require immediate patching across United States Government departments and agencies'.

## What it says, as the collector read it

#### NSA press release: Critical Patches Recommended for PHP Vulnerabilities

- Dated Oct. 21, 2026 (meta datePublished "Oct. 21, 2026"); no time given. The X UPDATE post linking to it went out 2026-10-22T00:43:58Z, so the release was presumably up by then.

- Captured 2026-10-22T08:09:54Z.

- Key verbatim lines:

- "vulnerabilities, collectively nicknamed “PHP2Shell,”"

- "PHP released their open-source software patch Wednesday, October 21, 2026."

- "All users should immediately conduct an inventory, where feasible, of hardware/software services reliant on now-vulnerable PHP versions."

- Mitigations: "Adding enhanced logging within network/operational environments"; "Replacing end-of-life PHP services which will not be compatible with patching"

- "If an immediate update is not feasible, it is recommended that organizations take the system offline until it can be fully mitigated."

- "“PHP2Shell” could allow attackers to take control of affected web servers without prior access or authentication."

- "These risks are serious enough to require immediate patching across United States Government departments and agencies."

- "NSA will continue to work with experts across the intelligence community (IC), including CISA"

- No CVE IDs are named in the release.
