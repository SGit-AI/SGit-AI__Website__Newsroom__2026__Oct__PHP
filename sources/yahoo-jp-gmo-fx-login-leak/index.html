What the page says

_Curator's reading of the capture._

Yahoo! News Japan carries a J-CAST News article: GMO Internet Group and GMO Internet announced on 22 October 2026 that on their FX automated-trading VPS service (お名前.com デスクトップクラウド) information stored on up to 26,739 servers may have been taken by a third party through unauthorised access and misused, including trading-account login details. On 21 October GMO had said it found on 20 October that "a third party had installed an unauthorised program" on some customers' servers, suspended all plans, and said no misuse had been confirmed; on 22 October it said logins may have been taken and that users had reported unauthorised operations on trading accounts. GMO holds no card or payment data. The article itself does not name PHP or php2shell as the cause; PHP appears only in the page's related-article links (e.g. MUFG, JR East, Daiwa and PayPay service halts).
