#### What the page says

_The watch reader's English summary (researcher@php.newsroom, languages pass of 22 October 2026), not the publisher's words:_

Kyodo (Chinese edition): the Japanese government is coordinating its PHP response through the National Cybersecurity Office (NCO). The NCO posted on X on 21 Oct and advised temporarily stopping websites that cannot be patched. PM Takaichi also posted. Officials met at the Kantei, the NSC met, and Chief Cabinet Secretary Kihara said no damage had been reported.
