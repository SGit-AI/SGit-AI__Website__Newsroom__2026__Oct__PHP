What the page says

_Curator's reading of the capture._

Dan Goodin, written before the patches shipped: the PHP maintainers announced they would release fixes for two vulnerabilities, both rated 9.2 out of 10, after purported details leaked. An unconfirmed report showed a video of an exploit tool called PHP2Shell running code on named WordPress, MediaWiki, phpBB, Magento, Joomla, Laravel, Symfony, Drupal and WHMCS versions. watchTowr said it had confirmed CVE-2026-109618 and CVE-2026-109619 are real; Sansec said it expected mass attacks within 15 minutes of the release.

> We reserve the right to release earlier should details of either vulnerability become public, or should evidence of active exploitation appear in the wild.
