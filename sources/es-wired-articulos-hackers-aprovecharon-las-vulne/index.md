#### What the page says

_Curator's reading of the capture._

WIRED en Espanol (Spanish), 22 October: hackers took advantage of the PHP vulnerabilities days before they were fixed. It reports that Amazon Web Services recorded attempts four days earlier, and that at least one actor started trying to exploit the flaws on 17 October, four days before the public disclosures; the first public proofs of concept began to appear on Wednesday, some apparently hours before the official disclosure; and the flaws are now said to be 'widely exploited'. A Spanish edition of the WIRED story already in the vault in English.
