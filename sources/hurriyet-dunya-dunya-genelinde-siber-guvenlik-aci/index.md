#### What the page says

_Curator's reading of the capture._

Hürriyet (Turkish): worldwide security hole in PHP; government sites were temporarily closed globally. It says security experts found the two flaws with the help of AI models; researchers at Calif, working with OpenAI, reported the first flaw on 11 August, and Alphabet's Wiz reported the second on 24 August. Wiz says its findings are among the most serious in PHP and probably in software history, that the underlying flaws had existed for decades, and that proving they were exploitable took a chain of steps at the edge of what human researchers could practically do.

> Güvenlik firması Calif'teki araştırmacılar, OpenAI ile çalışarak ilk açığı 11 Ağustos'ta bildirdi. Teknoloji devi Alphabet'e ait güvenlik firması Wiz ise ikinci açığı 24 Ağustos'ta raporladı.
