## What it says, as the collector read it

#### NCSC NZ alert

- "9:15AM, 22 October 2026" (NZDT, UTC+13, so 2026-10-21T20:15Z; our conversion). "(Updated 10:20am, 22-10-26)" = 21:20Z. TLP Clear. Captured 2026-10-22T08:17:00Z.

- **"The NCSC is aware of reports of active exploitation."** Every other agency says no exploitation has been seen (CISA, CCCS, ACSC in Australia, CERT.LV "no successful"). NZ does not say whose reports. Flag this as a discrepancy.

- Advice: "Update to PHP 8.5.12, 8.4.27, 8.3.36 or 8.2.35." / "apply the remediation as soon as possible" / "investigate unauthorised access or compromise". The update notes that firewalls, edge devices and embedded platforms need separate vendor remediation. No offline advice.
