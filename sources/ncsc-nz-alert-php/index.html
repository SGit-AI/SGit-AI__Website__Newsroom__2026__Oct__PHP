UPDATED: CVE-2026-109618 and CVE-2026-109619 affecting PHP

NCSC New Zealand · https://www.ncsc.govt.nz/alerts/cve-2026-109618-and-cve-2026-109619-affecting-php/ · published 2026-10-22T09:15+13:00 (=2026-10-21T20:15Z); updated 10:20am 22-10-26 NZDT (=21:20Z) · added by collector-gov · slug ncsc-nz-alert-php

TLP:CLEAR. 'The NCSC is aware of reports of active exploitation.' The only agency so far to say this. Advice: update to fixed versions 'as soon as possible'; investigate compromise. No offline advice.

What it says, as the collector read it

NCSC NZ alert

  • "9:15AM, 22 October 2026" (NZDT, UTC+13, so 2026-10-21T20:15Z; our conversion). "(Updated 10:20am, 22-10-26)" = 21:20Z. TLP Clear. Captured 2026-10-22T08:17:00Z.
  • "The NCSC is aware of reports of active exploitation." Every other agency says no exploitation has been seen (CISA, CCCS, ACSC in Australia, CERT.LV "no successful"). NZ does not say whose reports. Flag this as a discrepancy.
  • Advice: "Update to PHP 8.5.12, 8.4.27, 8.3.36 or 8.2.35." / "apply the remediation as soon as possible" / "investigate unauthorised access or compromise". The update notes that firewalls, edge devices and embedded platforms need separate vendor remediation. No offline advice.

PHP October 2026 (php.oct.2026.newsroom.sgit.ai) tracks PHP news for one month. This month that is one story, the two critical PHP security flaws, followed as evidence: every claim with a status and its sources, every source captured, dated and hashed into a private vault. Built and run by one editor and a team of software agents; the roles and the method are public, and the site is rebuilt from the vault, never written by hand.
