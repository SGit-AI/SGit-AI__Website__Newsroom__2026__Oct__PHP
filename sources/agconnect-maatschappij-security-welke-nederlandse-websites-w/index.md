#### What the page says

_The watch reader's English summary (researcher@php.newsroom, languages pass of 22 October 2026), not the publisher's words:_

AG Connect lists Dutch websites that went offline: at least 13 of 25 safety regions, the Schiphol app, the Port of Rotterdam, Odido's BEN and Simpel brands, many political parties, the Tweede Kamer and MijnOverheid, several hospitals, AG Connect itself and supermarket Hoogvliet. Most central government (Rijksoverheid) sites stayed up because they do not use PHP.

#### Verbatim quotes, with the watch reader's translation

Each quote is in the snapshot as served. The English is the source watch reader's translation (`translations/nl/news/agconnect/maatschappij-security-welke-nederlandse-websites-waren-uit-d.json`), not the publisher's and not checked word for word by the curator.

**number**: At least 13 of the 25 Dutch safety regions took their websites offline, including IJsselland, Twente, Gelderland-Midden, Noord-Holland-Noord, Rotterdam-Rijnmond and Zeeland.

> Zo gingen de websites van zeker dertien van de 25 veiligheidsregio's in Nederland offline.

_Watch reader's translation:_ Thus the websites of at least thirteen of the 25 safety regions in the Netherlands went offline.

**offline**: Schiphol took the Schiphol app offline as a precaution, while its website stayed up with a notice about a global digital threat.

> Schiphol haalde de Schiphol-app uit voorzorg uit de lucht.

_Watch reader's translation:_ Schiphol took the Schiphol app offline as a precaution.

**offline**: The Port of Rotterdam temporarily took its website offline, saying there were no signs of concrete security problems.

> Ook het Havenbedrijf Rotterdam heeft zijn website tijdelijk offline gehaald, maar gaf aan dat er geen signalen waren dat de situatie tot concrete veiligheidsproblemen leidde.

_Watch reader's translation:_ The Port of Rotterdam Authority also temporarily took its website offline, but indicated there were no signs that the situation led to concrete security problems.

**offline**: Telecom company Odido took the sites of its brands BEN and Simpel offline; Odido's own site does not use PHP.

> Telecombedrijf Odido heeft uit voorzorgeen de sites van de merken BEN en Simpel offline gehaald.

_Watch reader's translation:_ Telecom company Odido took the sites of the brands BEN and Simpel offline as a precaution.

**offline**: VVD, CDA, PRO, BBB and PvdD said their sites were taken offline preventively; D66, SGP, ChristenUnie, DENK and JA21 sites were also offline.

> VVD, CDA, PRO, BBB en PvdD hebben ook laten weten dat hun sites preventief offline zijn gehaald vanwege het risico.

_Watch reader's translation:_ VVD, CDA, PRO, BBB and PvdD have also announced that their sites were taken offline preventively because of the risk.

**offline**: Several hospital sites were unreachable, including Maastricht UMC+, Jeroen Bosch Ziekenhuis, BovenIJ, Albert Schweitzer Ziekenhuis, Haaglanden MC, St. Antonius and Elisabeth-TweeSteden.

> De sites van onder meer Maastricht UMC+, het Jeroen Bosch Ziekenhuis in Den Bosch, BovenIJ in Amsterdam en het Albert Schweitzer Ziekenhuis in Dordrecht waren niet bereikbaar.

_Watch reader's translation:_ The sites of, among others, Maastricht UMC+, the Jeroen Bosch Hospital in Den Bosch, BovenIJ in Amsterdam and the Albert Schweitzer Hospital in Dordrecht were not reachable.

**other**: Most Dutch central-government organisation websites kept working because they do not use PHP.

> De meeste websites van rijksoverheidsorganisaties werkten nog wel gewoon. Die maken geen gebruik van PHP.

_Watch reader's translation:_ Most websites of central government organisations still worked normally. They do not use PHP.

**offline**: Supermarket chain Hoogvliet took its website offline; AG Connect and sister titles such as iBestuur were also taken offline.

> Supermarktketen Hoogvliet haalde echter wel de website uit de lucht.

_Watch reader's translation:_ Supermarket chain Hoogvliet did, however, take its website offline.
