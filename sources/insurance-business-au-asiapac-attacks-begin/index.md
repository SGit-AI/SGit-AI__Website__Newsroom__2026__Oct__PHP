#### What the page says

_Curator's reading of the capture._

Insurance Business (Australia) on Asia-Pacific agency warnings. An update at 1pm AEDT on 22 October says security vendors report exploitation beyond honeypots "though official agencies have yet to confirm it": Hadrian sees PHP2Shell exploited at scale, mostly through PHP's session upload-progress path; Patchstack "has confirmed multiple active exploitation incidents"; Cloudflare reports early attacks; against that, Huntress had not observed exploitation across its partner systems. It also says watchTowr's decoy servers recorded attempts and "No breach at a real organisation had been confirmed by the time the fixes were released", that exploit code circulates and that the Dutch NCSC expects large-scale attacks. It adds the insurance angle: the memory-disclosure impact (Wiz) could make a data leak notifiable without any outage, and tells clients to get written confirmation of patching and to check unpatched servers for a break-in. No organisation is named as compromised.
