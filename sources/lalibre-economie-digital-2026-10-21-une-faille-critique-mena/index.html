_The watch reader's English summary (researcher@php.newsroom, languages pass of 22 October 2026), not the publisher's words:_

La Libre lists Belgian platforms taken offline: Flemish Region sites (on Minister-President Matthias Diependaele's order), the Flemish Parliament, FPS Finance informational sites, DPG Media and Roularta titles, Smartschool, Antwerp hospitals, VRT and the CCB's own site. _Watch reader's translation:_ FPS Finance's informational sites are also now temporarily unavailable (finances.belgium.be, ruling.be, garantiefonds, geregistreerdkassasysteem.be). My Minfin, which does not run on this technology, remains accessible.

offline: Roularta's magazine websites (Le Vif, Trends Tendances, Flair, Femmes d'aujourd'hui) were temporarily offline.

> Les sites web des magazines de l'éditeur Roularta (Le Vif, Trends Tendances, Flair, Femmes d'aujourd'hui, etc.) étaient également temporairement hors ligne.

_Watch reader's translation:_ The websites of publisher Roularta's magazines (Le Vif, Trends Tendances, Flair, Femmes d'aujourd'hui, etc.) were also temporarily offline.

offline: Smartschool, Antwerp hospital websites and broadcaster VRT's site were inaccessible on Wednesday.

> La plateforme pédagogique Smartschool était, elle aussi, inaccessible mercredi, pour les mêmes raisons, tout comme les sites internet d'hôpitaux anversois ou celui de la VRT.

_Watch reader's translation:_ The Smartschool education platform was also inaccessible on Wednesday for the same reasons, as were Antwerp hospital websites and VRT's.

offline: The Centre for Cybersecurity Belgium's own website was offline because of the flaw; the CCB advised those unable to patch to go offline.

> Le site internet du CCB lui-même est hors ligne en raison de cette faille de sécurité.

_Watch reader's translation:_ The CCB's own website is offline because of this security flaw.

offline: Amsterdam Schiphol suspended its mobile app as a precaution, and the Port of Rotterdam Authority took its web portal offline with no operational impact.

> l'aéroport d'Amsterdam Schiphol a temporairement suspendu son application mobile en raison de cette faille. Son site web reste en revanche accessible pour l'instant. L'Autorité portuaire de Rotterdam a temporairement mis son portail internet hors ligne
