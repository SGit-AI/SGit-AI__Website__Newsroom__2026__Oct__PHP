CNBC TV18, 23 October: India ranks 11th among the 18 countries most affected by PHP2Shell attacks, with an impact rate of 14% against a global average of 10%, according to Check Point Research. Hungary had the highest rate, 38% of organisations affected since disclosure, then Denmark 32% and Austria 28%. By sector, telecommunications was highest at 20%, then education 18%, and media and entertainment and associations and non-profits 16% each.
