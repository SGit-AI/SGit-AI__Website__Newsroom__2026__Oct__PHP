#### What the page says

_Curator's reading of the capture._

QNAP's press statement on PHP2Shell (CVSS v4.0 9.2, patched by PHP on 21 October). QNAP PSIRT launched a cross-functional response and is reviewing all products and cloud services. It states that QNAP PSIRT "has observed scanning activity and exploitation attempts targeting these vulnerabilities in the wild", and that it "detected and successfully blocked attempted attacks against QNAP's official website and online store, as well as its cloud services". It points to advisory QSA-26-18 for products.
