#### What the page says

_Curator's reading of the capture._

Sankei reports that DMM.com's entertainment site DMM announced temporary maintenance across its services on 21 October to respond to a vulnerability found in external software it uses, and that no leak of users' personal data occurred. Services were stopped in turn from 22:00 on 21 October; at 10:00 on 22 October some were still in maintenance. Asked by Sankei, DMM said it "cannot say more than the announcement" and did not say whether this related to the PHP problem.

> 同社は産経新聞の取材に、「発表文以上のことは言えない」と話し、21日にシステムの重大な欠陥に当たる脆弱性が見つかったプログラミング言語「PHP」を巡る問題との関連については明らかにしなかった。

_Curator's translation:_ Asked by the Sankei Shimbun, the company said it "cannot say more than the announcement" and did not clarify any connection with the problem around the programming language PHP, in which a serious vulnerability was found on the 21st.
