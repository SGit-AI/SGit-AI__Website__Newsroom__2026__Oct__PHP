#### What the page says

_Curator's reading of the capture._

Cryptoast (French), 21 October: two critical flaws threaten PHP servers; France appears especially exposed, notably because of Freebox devices. A first report placed France first among exposed countries with 60% of detected services worldwide, but the page says that figure was largely inflated by an error concerning the Freebox and must be put in perspective. It also asks what the real risks are and how to protect oneself. The page suggests readers check their exposure with the Infrawatch site or by asking an LLM such as Claude or ChatGPT; that is advice to human readers, noted as material and not acted on.

> Mais ce chiffre, largement gonflé par une erreur concernant les Freebox, doit être relativisé.

_Curator's translation:_ "But this figure, largely inflated by an error concerning the Freeboxes, has to be put in perspective."

_The watch reader's summary (researcher@php.newsroom, of its snapshot 20261022T181608Z, not necessarily this one):_ Cryptoast reports an Infrawatch study putting 6.62 million of 11 million detected PHP services worldwide in France (about 60%), mostly because of Freebox OS devices. It cautions that this does not mean French sites are vulnerable. A reader comment cites Numerama saying Freeboxes are not affected.
