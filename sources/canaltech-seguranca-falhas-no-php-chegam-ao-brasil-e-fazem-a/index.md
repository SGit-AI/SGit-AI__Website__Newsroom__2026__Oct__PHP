#### What the page says

_The watch reader's English summary (researcher@php.newsroom, languages pass of 22 October 2026), not the publisher's words:_

Canaltech reports that the PHP flaws reached Brazil. Anatel took its SEI electronic-process system offline, federal courts TRF1, TRF4 (Justiça Federal da 4ª Região) and TRF6 suspended external access or deadlines, and the federal CTIR Gov issued an official alert.

#### Verbatim quotes, with the watch reader's translation

Each quote is in the snapshot as served. The English is the source watch reader's translation (`translations/br/news/canaltech/seguranca-falhas-no-php-chegam-ao-brasil-e-fazem-anatel-e-tr.json`), not the publisher's and not checked word for word by the curator.

**offline**: Anatel, Brazil's telecoms regulator, confirmed to Canaltech that it took its SEI (electronic information system) offline because of the PHP vulnerabilities.

> A Agência Nacional de Telecomunicações (Anatel) confirmou ao Canaltech que retirou temporariamente do ar seu Sistema Eletrônico de Informações (SEI) por causa das vulnerabilidades.

_Watch reader's translation:_ The National Telecommunications Agency (Anatel) confirmed to Canaltech that it temporarily took its Electronic Information System (SEI) offline because of the vulnerabilities.

**official**: Anatel stated the outage is not an SEI flaw but is due to the PHP vulnerability, and warned that Moodle and Joomla systems could also be affected.

> "O caso não se trata de uma falha ou vulnerabilidade inerente ao SEI. A indisponibilidade temporária do serviço está relacionada a uma vulnerabilidade recente identificada no próprio PHP", explicou a agência.

_Watch reader's translation:_ "The case is not a flaw or vulnerability inherent to SEI. The temporary unavailability of the service is related to a recent vulnerability identified in PHP itself," the agency explained.

**offline**: Federal court TRF1 suspended external access to SEI, following a recommendation from TRF4; no restoration time given.

> O Tribunal Regional Federal da 1ª Região (TRF1) confirmou que suspendeu temporariamente o acesso externo ao SEI em razão das vulnerabilidades do PHP.

_Watch reader's translation:_ The Federal Regional Court of the 1st Region (TRF1) confirmed it temporarily suspended external access to SEI because of the PHP vulnerabilities.

**offline**: The federal courts of the 4th Region (Rio Grande do Sul, Santa Catarina, Paraná) cut external access to their systems from 11:00 on Wednesday.

> Já a Justiça Federal da 4ª Região, que atende Rio Grande do Sul, Santa Catarina e Paraná, anunciou a interrupção temporária do acesso externo aos seus sistemas a partir das 11h desta quarta-feira.

_Watch reader's translation:_ The Federal Justice of the 4th Region, which serves Rio Grande do Sul, Santa Catarina and Paraná, announced a temporary interruption of external access to its systems from 11am this Wednesday.

**offline**: TRF6 in Minas Gerais suspended court business and procedural deadlines across the state's federal courts (eproc, SEI, Moodle, portal and intranet affected), per O Fator.

> Em Minas Gerais, o TRF6 foi além e suspendeu o expediente e os prazos processuais em toda a Justiça Federal do estado, segundo reportagem do portal O Fator.

_Watch reader's translation:_ In Minas Gerais, TRF6 went further and suspended working hours and procedural deadlines across the entire Federal Justice of the state, according to a report by the portal O Fator.

**agency**: Brazil's CTIR Gov (Presidency's Institutional Security Cabinet) issued an official alert recommending immediate patching and, if impossible, suspending external access.

> O Centro de Prevenção, Tratamento e Resposta a Incidentes Cibernéticos de Governo (CTIR Gov), ligado ao Gabinete de Segurança Institucional da Presidência, também publicou um alerta oficial nesta quarta-feira (21).

_Watch reader's translation:_ The Government Cyber Incident Prevention, Handling and Response Centre (CTIR Gov), linked to the Presidency's Institutional Security Cabinet, also published an official alert this Wednesday (21st).
