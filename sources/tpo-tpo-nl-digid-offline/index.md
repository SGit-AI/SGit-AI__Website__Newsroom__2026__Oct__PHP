#### What the page says

_The watch reader's English summary (researcher@php.newsroom, languages pass of 22 October 2026), not the publisher's words:_

TPO reports that NotuBiz, used by about 100 municipalities, went down first, followed by the Dutch Data Protection Authority, the Union of Water Boards, UWV's sick-leave tool, RTL, ANP, the Schiphol app, hospitals and KWF; universities of applied sciences shut systems with SURF. **offline**: Meeting platform NotuBiz, used by about a hundred municipalities, went down first in the morning, followed by the Dutch Data Protection Authority, the Union of Water Boards, UWV's sick-reporting tool, RTL, ANP and the Schiphol app.

> Vergaderplatform Notubiz, dat door zo’n honderd gemeenten wordt gebruikt, ging ’s ochtends als eerste plat. Daarna volgden onder meer de Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens, de Unie van Waterschappen, de ziekmeldtool van UWV, RTL, het ANP en de app van Schiphol.

_Watch reader's translation:_ Meeting platform Notubiz, used by around a hundred municipalities, went down first in the morning. It was followed by, among others, the Dutch Data Protection Authority, the Union of Water Boards, UWV's sick-reporting tool, RTL, ANP and the Schiphol app.

**offline**: The Netherlands Association of Universities of Applied Sciences said many educational institutions switched off systems and are working on recovery with SURF; charity KWF also went offline.

> De Vereniging Hogescholen meldt dat “veel onderwijsinstellingen” systemen hebben uitgezet en samen met SURF werken aan herstel.

_Watch reader's translation:_ The Netherlands Association of Universities of Applied Sciences reports that 'many educational institutions' have switched off systems and are working on recovery together with SURF.

**offline**: In Belgium all websites of the Flemish government went offline, and in France those of the prefectures.

> In België gingen alle websites van de Vlaamse overheid uit de lucht, in Frankrijk die van de prefecturen en ook in Finland grepen overheden in.

_Watch reader's translation:_ In Belgium all websites of the Flemish government went offline, in France those of the prefectures, and in Finland too governments intervened.

**official**: NCSC-NL deputy director Corine Schipper-Derkse stressed it is not an attack but a design flaw in the software.

> “Het is niet zozeer een aanval, maar een weeffout die in de software zit en dat kan gebeuren”, aldus adjunct-directeur Corine Schipper-Derkse.

_Watch reader's translation:_ 'It is not so much an attack, but a design flaw in the software, and that can happen,' said deputy director Corine Schipper-Derkse.

**official**: Dutch Justice and Security Minister Van Weel said there were 'no signals yet' that the flaws were being actively exploited.

> Minister Van Weel van Justitie en Veiligheid liet weten dat er “nog geen signalen” zijn dat de lekken actief worden misbruikt.

_Watch reader's translation:_ Minister Van Weel of Justice and Security said there were 'no signals yet' that the flaws are being actively exploited.

**agency**: NCSC-NL speaks of a 'very high risk of complete takeover of the system'.

> Het NCSC spreekt van een “zeer hoog risico op volledige overname van het systeem”.

_Watch reader's translation:_ The NCSC speaks of a 'very high risk of complete takeover of the system'.
