# The flaws were found by researchers using frontier AI under OpenAI's Patch the Planet initiative

**Who says it:** BBC; OpenAI; Calif; Wiz · **Sources:** [BBC News](../../sources/bbc-live-ckgel5xp2xv9t/index.html) [OpenAI on X](../../sources/x-openai-php2shell/index.html) [Calif](../../sources/calif-php2shell/index.html) [Wiz](../../sources/wiz-blog-php2shell/index.html)

All three research parties say AI was used. Calif: 'No special harness, or access to models that weren't generally available to everyone were used'. Calif does not name a model; Wiz names Gemini 4 Argon, GPT-5.6 Sol and Wiz Atlas; OpenAI's blog reportedly names GPT-5.6 Sol but is blocked.
