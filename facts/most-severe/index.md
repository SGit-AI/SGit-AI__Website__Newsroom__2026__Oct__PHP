**Who says it:** BBC attributing Wiz · **Sources:** [BBC News](../../sources/bbc-live-ckgel5xp2xv9t/index.html) [Wiz](../../sources/wiz-blog-php2shell/index.html)

## Fact-check

**A2** Reported●●○○○○ opinion measures differently · Opinion · verdict: **holds in part**

The BBC attributes to Wiz that the flaws are the 'most severe' ever detected by AI; Wiz's own blog, which the claim cites, says something narrower ('among the most severe vulnerabilities discovered in PHP, and arguably in software history'). A Substack piece relays the WSJ quoting Wiz's co-founder with close wording, so the BBC line probably traces to an interview, not the blog. A reading, held by one party, reported second-hand.
