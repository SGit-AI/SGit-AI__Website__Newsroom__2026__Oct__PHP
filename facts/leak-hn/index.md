# The leak started with an anonymous Hacker News post citing a reliable source, which came down after screenshots spread; a screenshot of the 'PHP2Shell tool' credited to Calif.io and a short exploitation video also went up and came down

**Who says it:** Alexandre Daubois (PHP security team, personal blog) · **Sources:** [Daubois](../../sources/daubois-blog-php2shell-rumors/index.html)

Contradicts the Foundation's 'no technical details leaked' and The Hacker News's 'a proof-of-concept exploit leaked'. No copy of the post, screenshot or video is in the vault.

## Fact-check

**A4** Corroborated●●●●○○ disputed · Event · verdict: **holds in part**

The Hacker News post, its removal and the screenshots spreading are carried by Daubois and corroborated by Huntress, CybelAngel and Equixly (A4). 'Anonymous', 'citing a reliable source' and the screenshot 'credited to Calif.IO' are Daubois alone. Daubois says the screenshot 'leaked alongside the advisory', not that it came down; only the video 'went up and came down'.
