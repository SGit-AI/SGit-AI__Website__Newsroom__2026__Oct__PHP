(answer with the evidence window: logs reviewed from 17 October.) Did you take sites down? (precaution, not incident, as Oxford City Council put it.) Why did it take so long? (the fix was authored on 18 October and released under a coordinated plan that leaked.) - Attribution: three research teams and the PHP Foundation each tell the discovery story differently; do not pick one. The flaw was seen in 2024 and not fixed because exploitation could not be shown. - Point at primary sources: php.net's advisory and your national agency.
