# The specialist summary: CVE-2026-109618 and CVE-2026-109619 The technical summary of the two PHP flaws of October 2026: what is established with its sources, the six scale figures, the open questions and the last 48 hours. The story · for specialists # The specialist summary: php2shell, CVE-2026-109618 and CVE-2026-109619 On 21 October 2026 at 14:00 UTC the PHP project released fixes for two critical flaws, **CVE-2026-109618** and **CVE-2026-109619**, nicknamed *php2shell*. Either can give an unauthenticated attacker remote code execution on a web server running PHP, the language behind WordPress, Drupal, Wikipedia and a large share of the web. The flaws were found with AI help, the coordinated disclosure broke down two days early, and by the morning of 22 October the NSA, CISA and the UK's NCSC were telling organisations that could not patch to take systems offline.
