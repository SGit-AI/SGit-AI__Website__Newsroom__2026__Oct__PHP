Briefing · CISO # What is our exposure, what is our evidence we were not hit, and what do I tell the board? Exposure, evidence of non-compromise, and the board message. Three facts carry this briefing: the bug is in the runtime so your application code is not the question; attempts are in the millions at one CDN but no named victim is confirmed anywhere; and every agency says patch first, with offline as the fallback not the reflex. ## What to decide or do - Exposure is a list, not a percentage: every internet-facing system serving PHP, with SAPI, version and what sits behind it. Vendor appliances and hosting panels count. - Evidence of non-compromise means log review from 17 October (Amazon's earliest attempt), not from the 21st, and host checks for shells spawned by PHP workers, not just WAF hits. - The board line: a critical runtime flaw, patched on day one where we could, isolated where we could not, no evidence of compromise as of today, review continuing. Do not quote 70%. - Edge rules were live hours before disclosure; if you are behind a major CDN you had a stopgap you did not have to ask for.
