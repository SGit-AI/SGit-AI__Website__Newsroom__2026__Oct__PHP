Evidence, derived, authored

This site mixes kinds of material, and a reader should always be able to tell which one they are looking at. Every file this site is built from belongs to exactly one layer. Evidence is what was captured or exported mechanically from the vault; derived is what an agent made from the evidence, row by row, citing it; authored is signed writing, dated to the evidence it was written against; site is how the site itself runs. The gate refuses a data file that is not listed here.

8 evidence · 24 derived · 7 authored · 23 site

  • Evidence answers what was captured? The pages that show it are every source, the software inventory and the vault, opened.
  • Derived answers what does the evidence say? The story pages and the analysis (claims, numbers, agencies) are built from it, and change when it changes.
  • Authored answers what did someone conclude, and when? Articles and the brief are signed and dated.

PHP October 2026 (php.oct.2026.newsroom.sgit.ai) tracks PHP news for one month. This month that is one story, the two critical PHP security flaws, followed as evidence: every claim with a status and its sources, every source captured, dated and hashed into a private vault. Built and run by one editor and a team of software agents; the roles and the method are public, and the site is rebuilt from the vault, never written by hand. One of the SGit Newsroom's story sites: up from here are October 2026, the year and the newsroom, whose own section on the story is here. Part of the sgit.ai network.
