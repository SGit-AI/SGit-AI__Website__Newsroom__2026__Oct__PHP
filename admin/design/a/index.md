### Three questions to put to your team this week
 from your briefing 

1 

#### Are we exposed?

Ask for the list, not a percentage: every system that runs PHP, including vendor products and hosting panels, and which are on the internet. 

2 

#### Is everything updated and restarted?

Updating is not enough until PHP is restarted. Ask for the running version, checked after the restart, system by system. 

3 

#### Who can pull a system offline, and how fast?

Every agency puts offline last, but you need to be able to do it in minutes. Name the person and rehearse it once.
