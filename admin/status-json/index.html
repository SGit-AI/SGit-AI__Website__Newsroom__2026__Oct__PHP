status.json: this site's state, for other sites

One small file at the site root, /status.json, that another site or an agent can read instead of scraping pages: the story's status, the version, when the evidence was last checked, the counts, three headline facts with their status and sources, and the latest story. The SGit Newsroom's front page reads it while the story is developing, and its tracker checks for it before the newsroom's own section on the story becomes a pointer here.

The rules

  • Fields are only ever added. A reader of version 1 keeps working.
  • A headline fact is a claim id from every claim; its text and status are the claim's, never rewritten for the feed.
  • data_as_of is the time of the latest capture in the evidence vault, not the time of the build.
  • No personal data, no credential, nothing that is not already on a page.

PHP October 2026 (php.oct.2026.newsroom.sgit.ai) tracks PHP news for one month. This month that is one story, the two critical PHP security flaws, followed as evidence: every claim with a status and its sources, every source captured, dated and hashed into a private vault. Built and run by one editor and a team of software agents; the roles and the method are public, and the site is rebuilt from the vault, never written by hand. One of the SGit Newsroom's story sites: up from here are October 2026, the year and the newsroom, whose own section on the story is here. Part of the sgit.ai network.
