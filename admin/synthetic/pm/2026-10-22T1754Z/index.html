What works

  • The thirty-second answer is exactly the stand-up summary I need: unplanned work this week, possible downtime, 8.1 and older becomes a migration, ask each owner for a patch date and a test plan. (briefings/pm/index.html)
  • 'What to decide or do' is six numbered actions in a sensible order that map straight onto a plan: inventory, date per system, regression budget, downtime decision, migration project, second round. (briefings/pm/index.html)
  • The datacentre-move framing (inventory, owner, date, test) gives me a planning shape I can explain to forty clients. (briefings/pm/index.html)
  • Numbered steps 0 to 6 with the agencies behind each step shown as chips; I can cite the source of every line of my plan. (playbook/index.html)
  • 'Decide who can make that call and how fast it can be executed, and write it down before you need it' is a clear action for me, not just for engineers. (playbook/index.html)
  • The section on how a fix reaches a server (patched for you, rebuild and redeploy, OS update, extended support, hosting panel, waiting on supplier) lets me sort client sites into workstreams by hosting type. (patching/index.html)
  • A short, pasteable answer with a URL for the 'we are on 7.4' question; I can forward it to a client as is. (questions/old-php/index.html)
  • 'Last checked 22 October 2026, 11:34 UTC' in the header and UTC times throughout make the currency of the information unambiguous. (index.html)

What does not

  • high No deadline anywhere. The guidance says 'now', 'this week' and 'the next fortnight' but never states whether any agency set a due date, so I cannot put a hard date on the plan or tell a client how late is too late. (briefings/pm/index.html)
    'Expect unplanned work this week' and 'Keep a slot free in the next fortnight'; the playbook steps carry no dates at all.
  • high Nothing on how to report progress, although the briefing's own question promises 'what do I tell stakeholders'. There is no status template, no suggested measures (systems inventoried, patched, verified, offline) and no wording for a client update. (briefings/pm/index.html)
    'What this briefing covers' lists 'what to tell stakeholders about downtime', but the page body only says 'Agree with stakeholders now who can make that call'.

What this reader would change, in order

  1. Add a 'Deadlines' line at the top of the PM briefing stating whether any agency set a due date (and that CISA has not listed these flaws in KEV, so no federal due date), plus a recommended internal target such as internet-facing patched within N days. (briefings/pm/index.html, small)
    I plan against dates; 'this week' and 'next fortnight' cannot go in a programme plan or a client SLA conversation.
  2. Add a 'How to report progress' section with a copyable status table (system, owner, PHP version, hosting route, patched yes/no, verified running version, offline yes/no, next date) and a two-line stakeholder update template. (briefings/pm/index.html, medium)
    Reporting is half my question and the briefing title promises it; with forty clients I need one consistent format.
  3. Add a 'Changed in the last 24 hours' block at the top of the timeline and the PM briefing, listing only rows added or changed since the previous day. (timeline/index.html, medium)
    My daily routine is 'what moved since yesterday'; the read-tracking bar does not help on a first visit or on a new device.
  4. Add a 'Who does this' label to each playbook step (inventory owner, host or platform, developer, security, decision-maker). (playbook/index.html, small)
    Lets me turn the steps straight into assigned tasks instead of translating each one.
  5. Make the PM briefing actions a downloadable or copyable checklist with tick boxes. (briefings/pm/index.html, small)
    I would drop it straight into our tracker for each client.
  6. Fix or explain the cPanel row under the 7.4, 8.0 and 8.1 branches so the package names match the claim that cPanel backported to those versions. (patching/index.html, small)
    Agency client sites are mostly on panels; I need to know which clients wait on cPanel and which on Plesk.
  7. Rewrite the playbook's opening sentence on where 'offline' falls into one plain line, for example 'Most agencies put taking a site offline last; CERT-EU, Canada and France put isolation earlier.' (playbook/index.html, small)
    The order is what I am planning; a sentence I had to read three times undermines the list.
  8. Label or remove the '£5.00' header badge. (index.html, small)
    An unexplained falling money figure distracts and makes me doubt the site.
