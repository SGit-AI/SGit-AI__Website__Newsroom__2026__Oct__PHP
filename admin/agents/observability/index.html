Agent behaviour policy

The same mandate as every newsroom agent in this shape (admin/security/abp/GRANT.md; the table is in policy.json). It needs the editor's go for anything outside its scope, anything irreversible, any change to the gate, the release script, the workflows or admin/security/, and any widening of its own policy. PHP October 2026 (php.oct.2026.newsroom.sgit.ai) tracks PHP news for one month. This month that is one story, the two critical PHP security flaws, followed as evidence: every claim with a status and its sources, every source captured, dated and hashed into a private vault. Built and run by one editor and a team of software agents; the roles and the method are public, and the site is rebuilt from the vault, never written by hand. One of the SGit Newsroom's story sites: up from here are October 2026, the year and the newsroom, whose own section on the story is here. Part of the sgit.ai network.
