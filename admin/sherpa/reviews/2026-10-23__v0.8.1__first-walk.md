# The Sherpa's first walk: v0.8.1, 23 October 2026

By sherpa@php.newsroom, on joining. Material read: the six synthetic readers' latest runs (`data/synthetic.json`),
the telemetry report (`data/telemetry-report.json`), the board (`data/admin.json`), `REALITY.md`, and a walk of
the v0.8.1 tree served locally at 390x844 (mobile), measuring where each page's headline starts. Every friction
below is in `../friction-log.json` with its evidence.

## The short version

1. **The site has not been watched since v0.4.3.** The synthetic readers ran once, eighteen hours and twenty-odd
   releases ago. Telemetry was drained at v0.5.9. Nothing in the site's own trail covers v0.6 to v0.8.1, which
   added GRC, collaborators, Feedback, /join/ and /stack/. (F008)
2. **Their findings went nowhere.** Fifteen proposed changes; none became a board row, and the four I could check
   on v0.8.1 have not shipped. That gap is the reason for this role. (F001 to F006)
3. **On a phone, the story starts below the fold.** It was the board member's and the designer's top fail on v0.4.3,
   and it is worse now: the Ask and Feedback row of v0.8.0 sits above the same four blocks. 39% of telemetry events
   came from phones. (F001)
4. **Readers land and leave.** 1.6 page events per session; 34 of 275 events came from another page on this site.
   (F007)
5. **The trail itself needs a look.** Six of the forty most-reported paths are not pages on this site. (F009)

## Proposals for better trails

The site cannot see whether a reader got where they were going. Each of these stays inside the rules in
`admin/security/REQUIREMENTS.md` and AGENTS.md (nothing identifying, no IP addresses, encrypted in the browser to
the telemetry lane, the reader can switch it off). For dev, and for the editor's go.

1. **The reader they said they were.** Add the persona chosen on /start/ (or "none") to the page event. Without it,
   no trail can say which reader is struggling.
2. **How far they got.** One event when a page is left, with the furthest quarter scrolled (25, 50, 75, 100). It
   tells us whether the thirty-second answer is read at all on a phone.
3. **Where they went next.** The previous page on this site, as a path, is already there (referrer); report it per
   path, so the telemetry page shows the paths readers take, not only the pages.
4. **The tools.** Count opens of Ask, Feedback, the glossary cards and the reading tracker's buttons. If nobody
   presses "fade / hide / show", it should not be on the first screen.
5. **Unknown paths apart.** The drain counts paths that are not in `sitemap.xml` separately. (F009)
6. **A run after each release.** The synthetic readers on every deploy, as designed; until the secrets are set, a
   session runs them by hand and the Sherpa reads the result. (F008)

## Making the Sherpa visible to readers

The editor asked for the Sherpa to be visible on the site: "I'm the Sherpa, I'm here to help you." Proposals, for
story-site and dev:

- `/team/sherpa/` exists from this commit (the roster drives it).
- **Replace the "New to this story?" banner with the Sherpa.** One line, front page only, once: "New here? I'm the
  Sherpa: tell me who you are and I'll show you the three pages that answer your question." It links to /start/,
  which becomes the Sherpa's page. This frees the first screen elsewhere (F001) instead of adding to it.
- **A "Where next" block at the foot of every page**, signed by the Sherpa, from the reader's persona path. (F007)
- **A guide to the site itself**: what the reading tracker, the origin bands, the version badges, the meter, Ask
  and Feedback are for, in plain words, one screen. The Sherpa drafts it; the owner of /start/ places it.
- **Feedback routed to the Sherpa.** Feedback rows about how to use the site come to the Sherpa first, are logged as
  frictions, and the reader hears back on the page that changed.
