Zooming in: the fractal layers

  • A CVE opens the code. The two CVE nodes open the review graph of the fix, which has its own ontology (the flaws, the design of the patch, the files and functions changed) and its own verbs, joined to this one by a named bridge. This is the estate's test of a fractal graph: going down opens a new vocabulary under the same grammar.
  • A source opens its captures, and each capture lists its files with their SHA-256. A claim appears in a capture where the fact-check desk found its words there. This step is addressing rather than a new ontology, and is described as such.
  • A party opens what it said and what it passed on: the claims and numbers it states, the ones it relays, the sources it publishes, the organisation it is part of. Every party.
  • A party steps across into the risk graph. The risk desk keeps its own graph (the risk graph), where parties appear as stakeholders, mostly as classes (WAF vendors, local councils, national governments). data/crosswalk.json declares the bridges: a party is a stakeholder, or is among a class of them; a CVE is the same anchor in all three graphs. Bridges, not a merge: each graph keeps its words.
  • A tension opens the claims it flags: the contradiction ledger of the fact-check desk, one node per row, because two claims that disagree are not one edge with the same name both ways.

Verbs this graph will not use, and why:

  • related_to: a generic association edge says nothing; the estate bans it outright
  • mentions: say what the mention does: cites, names, states or relays
  • associated_with: generic
  • contradicts: self-inverse; a contradiction is a node that flags both claims
  • measures_differently: self-inverse; recorded as a contradiction node with tension 'measures-differently'
  • agrees_with: self-inverse, and agreement is read from independent states edges, not asserted

The grammar is the estate's, from graphs.sgit.ai; the files are data/ontology.json (kinds, verbs, taxonomies, formulas), data/entities.json (one node per party, whatever a page calls it) and data/attribution.json (who states each row). The graph is data/graph.json. Kept by the cartographer. The component that draws it is documented for every agent on the graph component page.

PHP October 2026 (php.oct.2026.newsroom.sgit.ai) tracks PHP news for one month. This month that is one story, the two critical PHP security flaws, followed as evidence: every claim with a status and its sources, every source captured, dated and hashed into a private vault. Built and run by one editor and a team of software agents; the roles and the method are public, and the site is rebuilt from the vault, never written by hand.
