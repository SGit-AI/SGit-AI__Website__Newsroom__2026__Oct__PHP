# Your desk: what is new to you, and what changed since you read it

This site keeps track of what you have read, in your browser, so the next visit costs you only what changed. Mark an item read (the circle beside it), or the whole page, or everything at once: from then on you see only what is new, and anything that changes after you read it comes back marked as changed, with a link to the change. The **⋯** beside any item takes a decision (read later, follow up, not for me) or a note of your own: a correction, a comment, an idea.

Your desk needs JavaScript: it is built in your browser from what this browser has kept.

## Where this is kept

Only in this browser: what you read is under `p2s.reader.v1`, your decisions and notes under `sgit.notes.v1` in the same record format as [newsroom.sgit.ai](https://newsroom.sgit.ai/), the feeds you add under `p2s.feeds.v1`. Nothing is sent anywhere. Export your desk to keep it or to move it to another browser; the next step, from the newsroom, is to carry it into a vault of your own, encrypted, across your devices. How the site decides what is new and changed is in [the brief](../admin/briefs/index.html#2026-10-22__feeds-the-delta-and-subscriptions).
