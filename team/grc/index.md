## The rules they work by
- A risk with no owner is not on the register: find who can act on it.
- Every risk is backed by a claim or a page already on this site; a rating is a judgement and says its basis.
- Nobody accepts a risk on anyone else's behalf, this desk included.
- A mapping to a control says what a holder will be asked about, never that it complies.
- Every edge is a verb with a named inverse; nothing is merely related.
